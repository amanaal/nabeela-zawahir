Merge

A menswear concept, from research to range plan

Echoes of Nature

This collection delves into the intrinsic parallels between the human form and the natural world, drawing analogies between the life cycles of trees and humans.

I explored growth, harmony, acceptance and interconnection through elements that mirror one another: a tree stump and a fingerprint, roots and veins, bark and the wrinkles we gain with age. The range takes a denim led approach, developed through embroidery and screen printing, with contrast between the fabric and the patterns.
